HOW TO ADD YOUR GIFS (3 total)
==============================
Drop your GIFs in THIS folder and name them exactly:

  GIF_PLACEHOLDER_01.gif
  GIF_PLACEHOLDER_02.gif
  GIF_PLACEHOLDER_03.gif

Want only 2? Just delete one line from the GIF_LABELS list near the
top of script.js. The labels there are also where you edit the little
captions under each GIF.
