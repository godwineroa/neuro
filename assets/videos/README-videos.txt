HOW TO ADD YOUR VIDEOS (3 total)
=================================
Create a folder named "videos" inside /assets/ and drop your clips
there, named exactly:

  VIDEO_PLACEHOLDER_01.mp4
  VIDEO_PLACEHOLDER_02.mp4
  VIDEO_PLACEHOLDER_03.mp4

They appear as polaroids on the "My Treasures" wall with a ▶ badge.
Hovering one gives a silent preview; tapping it opens a full player
with sound and controls.

Tips:
- MP4 (H.264) works everywhere. Keep clips short & under ~20 MB
  each so the page stays fast.
- Want more or fewer videos? Edit the VIDEO_CAPTIONS list near the
  top of script.js — the wall rebuilds itself automatically.
- The captions under each video are also edited in VIDEO_CAPTIONS.
